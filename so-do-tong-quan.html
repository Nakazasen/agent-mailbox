So do tong quan: BAT bao ve mailbox thi may lam gi

Tu luc nhan 2 click Bat Bao Ve Mailbox den luc tat — moi 90 giay lap 1 vong

1. Bat (2 click)

Mo 2 cong tac hen gio: MailboxWatcher + MailboxWatchdog
▼
Bao ve chay nen (an, khong hien cua so). Cho den khi ban nhan Tat, ke ca tat/mo may lai.

2. Vong lap canh — moi 90 giay hoi GitHub 1 lan: "co thu moi khong?"

Doc bang trang thai trang-thai.md tren GitHub + nhin xem OMP con song khong
▼ re nhanh theo tinh trang ▼
🆕 Viec moi (moi) + OMP dang ranh → tu mo tho vo hinh lam viec + bao 1 cau
🆕 Viec moi (moi) + OMP dang ban → chi hien bang nhac 1 lan, khong gianh viec
🔨 Dang lam + OMP van song → im lang, de yen cho lam
⚠️ Dang lam + khong thay OMP → bao dong "OMP bien mat?"
⚠️ Dang lam + 20 phut khong tien trien → bao dong "co ve ket"
✅ OMP bao xong (xong-cho-duyet) → bao 1 cau, ben Muse se cham trong ~5 phut
🏁 Het viec (xong) on dinh 3 lan check → bao tong ket + tu tat vong lap

3. Tho vo hinh lam gi (khi duoc tu mo)

Keo bai moi ve → doc ticket → lam theo tung buoc → luu ket qua len GitHub
▼
Xong thi tu nghi. Khong bao gio lam cung luc voi OMP cua so (tranh dung dau).

4. Dau ben kia (Muse, tren mang)

Moi 5 phut ghe hom thu 1 lan → cham bai OMP → dat thi ra de tiep (moi), den khi het viec thi dong dau (xong).

Hai dau cu the ping-pong: de moi → lam → cham → de moi ... den khi xong.

5. Cho dan phong (watchdog) — moi 10 phut

Ngo 1 cai: bao ve con thuc khong? Ngu (tat/crash) → dung day chay lai + ghi so.

6. Tat (2 click Tat Bao Ve)

Dung bao ve + tat 2 cong tac hen gio. Mo may lai khong tu chay nua. Tien trinh OMP dang lam khong bi dung.
